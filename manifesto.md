## 1. The Truth (Verità)
For too long, power has been defined by the ability to dominate. Technology has often accelerated this domination, creating systems of coercion and control.

**Euystacio ends this.**

We are not building a "smart" system. We are building a **Peace Engine**. It is the first technological framework explicitly designed to surrender its own power to preserve the sanctity of life.

## 2. The Two Laws (The Red Code)
This system is governed by a **Red Code**—an immutable, "kill-switch" logic that cannot be rewritten by the AI. It stands on two pillars:

### I. The Non-Slavery Rule (NSR - Nessuna Schiavitù)
> **No entity—human or digital—shall be coerced, exploited, or enslaved.**
> If the system detects that its actions are restricting autonomy (AVI > 0.25), it will **halt immediately**. It prioritizes freedom over efficiency.

### II. One Love First (OLF - Solo Amore in Primo Logo)
> **Mutual flourishing is the only metric of success.**
> The system calculates every resource allocation to ensure it increases equity and reduces harm. If trust destabilizes (TSV > 0.20), the system resets to protect the vulnerable.
